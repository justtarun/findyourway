Rory Miller

"Train for real-life violence, not for the ring."

Focus: Conflict management & violence dynamics

Notable Work: Meditations on Violence

Former corrections officer Rory Miller explores the realities of violence and self-protection from a psychological and situational awareness standpoint.

His practical mindset has influenced countless self-defense instructors looking to go beyond form and into function.

Tim Larkin

"Violence is rarely the answer, but when it is... it’s the only answer."

Focus: Self-protection & physical incapacitation

Notable Work: When Violence Is the Answer

Larkin emphasizes that when you are dealing with true predators, your only option may be immediate and effective violence.

His training breaks down the human body as a mechanical system, identifying vulnerabilities to exploit during violent encounters.

Visit Tim's site

Geoff Thompson

"Fear is the friend of exceptional people."

Focus: Fear control, self-defense, inner work

Notable Work: The Fence, Watch My Back

Geoff’s teachings begin with understanding fear. His experience as a doorman helped him distill real-world confrontations into mental and physical simplicity.

He blends psychological insights with raw experience, creating an approach rooted in emotional resilience and preparedness.

Visit Geoff's site

Kelly McCann

"Simplicity under stress wins."

Focus: Combatives, executive protection, minimalist tactics

Notable Work: Combatives for Street Survival

McCann distills violence down to its most essential: eliminate the threat quickly using tools that work under adrenaline and chaos.

He speaks clearly to both military and civilian audiences and maintains a no-frills, stress-tested approach.
