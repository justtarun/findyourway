Find Your Way

Explore the philosophies of modern martial artists forging their own paths. Take inspiration, learn their approaches, and craft your own unique expression.

Rory Miller

"Train for real-life violence, not for the ring."

Focus: Real-world violence and conflict management.

Notable Work: Meditations on Violence

More info...

Rory Miller is a former corrections officer with extensive real-world experience in violence dynamics. He emphasizes training for reality rather than for sport, breaking down how conflict actually plays out and what psychological preparation is needed. His writing is approachable and often challenges traditional martial dogma.

Tim Larkin

"Violence is rarely the answer, but when it is... it’s the only answer."

Focus: Target Focus Training, self-protection.

Notable Work: When Violence is the Answer

More info...

Tim Larkin teaches that you cannot reason with someone intent on harming you. His method centers on disabling an opponent's ability to continue aggression as quickly as possible using simple, brutal mechanics. His courses are especially focused on survival scenarios.

Geoff Thompson

"Fear is the friend of exceptional people."

Focus: Reality-based self-defense and fear management.

Notable Work: The Fence, Watch My Back

More info...

Once a nightclub doorman, Geoff Thompson developed a reputation for honesty about fear, violence, and the mental process of confrontation. He encourages internal work and emotional clarity as the basis for any physical readiness. His blend of martial realism and self-awareness is unique in the field.

Kelly McCann

"Simplicity under stress wins."

Focus: Combatives and military-grade personal security tactics.

Notable Work: Combatives for Street Survival

More info...

Kelly McCann, a veteran of both military and law enforcement, teaches realistic, no-nonsense combat tactics with a focus on efficiency. He emphasizes simplicity and decisiveness, believing that when faced with life-or-death situations, keeping things simple is the key to survival.
