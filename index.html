Rory Miller

Approach: Conflict strategy, real-world violence.

Key work: Meditations on Violence.

Essence: Train for what actually happens, not fantasy.

Tim Larkin

Approach: Target-focused training (TFT).

Key work: When Violence is the Answer.

Essence: Violence can be a tool for protection, not emotion.

Geoff Thompson

Approach: Reality-based self defense, fear control.

Key work: The Fence, Watch My Back.

Essence: Awareness and managing fear is as important as technique.

Kelly McCann

Approach: Combatives, military background.

Key work: Crucible Training, Urban Combatives.

Essence: Fast, simple, effective under pressure.
